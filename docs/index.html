Common help:

  • Setting up automatic tracking: open Settings in the app, tap “Set up Apple Wallet tracking,” and follow the steps to add the Wallet Import Shortcut.
  • Adding a purchase manually: tap the “+” on the Spending screen.
  • Setting a budget: Settings → Budget → Monthly Budget.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Wudget is built to keep your financial data private. Everything you track stays on your device. We don't run servers that store your data, and we never sell or share it.

Data stored on your device

Your transactions (amount, merchant, card name, date, category, and notes), your budget, and your preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to us or to any server.

Location

If you grant location permission, the app tags purchases imported from Apple Pay with their location so it can show them on a map. This location data is stored only on your device. You can deny or turn off location access at any time in iOS Settings, and location never leaves your device.

Apple Wallet and Apple Pay

The app cannot read your Apple Wallet or your cards. Transactions arrive only from a Shortcut you choose to set up, which passes along the amount, merchant, and card name you map. We have no access to your bank or card accounts.

No accounts or servers

Any sign-in information is kept on your device. We do not operate a backend that holds your personal information.

No third-party tracking

The app does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking, and does not send your data to third parties.

Children

Wudget is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above reflects the most recent version.
